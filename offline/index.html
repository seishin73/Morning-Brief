How stories are chosen. Every candidate is scored on five signals — how many independent outlets carry it, how recently it broke, the standing of the outlet, how well it fits the topic, and where it sat on the section front. Corroboration is weighted heaviest, so the tally marks beside each headline are close to the ranking itself. Near-duplicates are clustered before scoring, and no story runs twice inside a section or across two sections — Top of the brief is the one place a story repeats, the way a front page trails what is inside.
Sourcing. Headlines link to the original publication. Summaries are written from headlines, excerpts and reporting metadata; no article text is reproduced. Photographs are freely-licensed subject images from Wikimedia Commons except where a publisher photo is credited on the card.
